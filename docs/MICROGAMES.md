# Microgames: the agreed design sheet

Every round has its own arcade action; the beat is music and the fuse only. The player's YB
is the **party leader** trying to become PM, so no round puts a boss above them. Each round
starts from something that really happens in a Malaysian election, and any fact it shows must
be correct (see the pillars in `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/GAME_PLAN.md`).

Agreed with Axel on 1 Nov 2026. Status: ✅ built, ⬜ to build.

## Act 1: Nomination

| Round | Call | Action | What happens | Fact shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ Pecut SPR | PECUT! | Lane dodge | Dash through supporters to the nomination centre before 10:00 | Papers are accepted only 9–10am |
| ✅ Semak Borang | SEMAK! | Spot the mistake | Tap the one wrong field on each nomination form (age 17, no signature, no deposit receipt) | A candidate must be a citizen aged 18+ (Federal Constitution Art. 47) |
| ✅ Bayar Deposit | KIRA! | Count to exact | Drag RM1,000 / RM500 / RM100 bundles to pay exactly RM10,000; overpaying costs time. Pakar Kira shows a running total | RM10,000 for Parliament, lost below 1/8 of the votes; a separate RM5,000 campaign-material deposit |
| ✅ Cop Watikah | COP! | Stamp on the move | As leader, stamp the party seal in the box of each candidate's letter as it slides past; skip letters with a crossed-out name | Party candidates need the party's letter of authority to use its symbol |
| ✅ Rebut Kamera | SENYUM! | Step into the gap | Step into a gap in the group-photo line as the shutter fires; bumping someone is an "elbow" photo. From the second photo a latecomer waits behind one gap and steps in just before the click | (satire) |

## Act 2: Campaign

| Round | Call | Action | What happens | Fact shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ Goreng Kuey Teow | KACAU! | Hold to pose | Grab the senduk only when a camera flashes; let the hawker cook | (satire) |
| ✅ Perang Speaker | LAUNG! | Mash with heat | Shout into the rival's water breaks; overheat and the amp screeches | (satire) |
| ⬜ Turap Jalan | TURAP! | Trace the road | Roll the road over the potholes before the photo car arrives (could become *Tampal Lubang*, the one-bucket pothole photo-op) | (satire) |
| ✅ Susun Manifesto | JANJI! | Stacker | Drop promise blocks onto the manifesto tower; whatever overhangs breaks off as a broken promise; stack 8 | (satire) |
| ✅ Baling Risalah | EDAR! | Flick only leaflets | Flick leaflets to passers-by; gifts (cash envelope, rice) go in the bin | Giving money or gifts to influence a vote is bribery, food and drink is treating (Election Offences Act 1954) |
| ✅ Joget Trend | JOGET! | Copy the dance | A trend-setter shows a dance one pose a beat (arms up, lean left, lean right, squat); copy it in order by swipe, arrow keys or the pose buttons. Three dances (3, 4, 4 poses); a wrong pose replays the dance | GE15 was called the "TikTok election" |
| ⬜ Cuci Longkang | SENTAL! | Rub it clean | Rub muck patches clean; dirty patches smell and residents' complaints pile up; the reporter arrives when the fuse ends | (satire) |
| ✅ Pacak Bendera | PACAK! | Push your luck | Plant flags until just before 11:59pm, never inside the ring around the polling centre | Campaigning ends 11:59pm the night before polling; material within 50 m of a polling centre is an offence |

Susun Manifesto replaces Potong Reben (a one-swipe wait that wasn't fun). Joget Trend replaces the earlier Payung Bos idea (a boss doesn't fit the player as leader).

## Act 3: Polling day

| Round | Call | Action | What happens | Fact shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ Celup Dakwat Kekal | CELUP! | Steer, dip, hold | Dip the left index finger to the nail, avoiding the rim | General election: the left index finger |
| ⬜ Tapis Undi Rosak | BANTAH! | Object at the right moment | The KTM calls each ballot "Sah" or "Ditolak"; object only to a wrong call | Undi rosak (officially *kertas undi ditolak*): no official stamp, more than one candidate, identifying writing, or unmarked. Any clear mark counts, not only ✗. The KTM decides |
| ✅ Borang 14 | ASINGKAN! | Swipe sort | Sort the stream's ballots into piles | Form 14 is the count for one stream |

## Finale

| Round | Call | Action | What happens | Fact shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⬜ Rundingan Gabungan | RUNDING! | Juggle the calls | Accept or decline calls from fictional parties to reach 112 with only three ministries to offer | 222 seats; a simple majority is 112. No palace, no appointment scene |
